A Hydra/JavaScript code uses the Ethereum/Tezos crypto currency status to feed the visual development of each different piece in the collection.
ETH information used as variables:
1) price of Ether/Tezos in US$,
2) total trade volume for the last 24 hours,
3) past hour price change and
4) update time in the Unix timestamp format.
